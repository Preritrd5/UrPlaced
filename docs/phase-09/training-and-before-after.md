# UrPlaced — Phase 09: Training & Before/After Learning Evaluation

## 1. Context & Educational Methodology
Training interventions (workshops, bootcamps, masterclasses) are frequently deployed before campus hiring seasons. However, conventional placement portals either ignore training outcomes or merely report aggregate attendance.

**UrPlaced Phase 09** introduces rigorous **Paired Observation Before/After Learning Evaluation**:
- Distinguishes **registration** from **verified attendance**.
- Measures performance delta only on **paired attendee cohorts** (students who attended AND completed both baseline and follow-up assessments).
- Enforces explicit causal boundaries to prevent inflated claims of workshop effectiveness.

---

## 2. Enrollment vs Attendance Distinction
In `training_attendance`:
- `enrollment_status`: `ENROLLED` | `WAITLISTED` | `DROPPED`
- `attendance_status`: `ATTENDED` | `ABSENT` | `EXCUSED`

A student who registers but does not attend is categorized as `ABSENT` and strictly excluded from before/after paired calculations.

---

## 3. Mathematical Evaluation Rules
1. **Paired Ingestion:**
   Only attendees $s$ with valid scored attempts in both the baseline assessment $A_{\text{base}}$ and follow-up assessment $A_{\text{follow}}$ form the evaluation set $P$:
   $$P = \{ s \in \text{Attendees} \mid \exists \text{Attempt}(s, A_{\text{base}}) \land \exists \text{Attempt}(s, A_{\text{follow}}) \}$$
2. **Baseline Cohort Mean:**
   $$\bar{X}_{\text{base}} = \frac{1}{|P|} \sum_{s \in P} \text{Score}(s, A_{\text{base}})$$
3. **Follow-up Cohort Mean:**
   $$\bar{X}_{\text{follow}} = \frac{1}{|P|} \sum_{s \in P} \text{Score}(s, A_{\text{follow}})$$
4. **Absolute Delta:**
   $$\Delta_{\text{abs}} = \bar{X}_{\text{follow}} - \bar{X}_{\text{base}}$$
5. **Relative Percentage Delta:**
   $$\Delta_{\text{rel}} = \left(\frac{\Delta_{\text{abs}}}{\bar{X}_{\text{base}}}\right) \times 100$$

---

## 4. Mandatory Causal Invariant & Limitations Disclaimer
Every paired analysis generated by the engine and rendered on staff dashboards or exported reports must present the verbatim disclaimer:

> *"Correlation does not imply causation. Score improvements reflect observed performance differences between the baseline and follow-up assessments for paired attendees. Performance changes may also be influenced by self-study, repeated exposure, or test variation."*
